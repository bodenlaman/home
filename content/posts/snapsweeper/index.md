## Development Process
# Inspiration
As an avid photographer, I suddenly found myself with tends of thousands of photos, unwilling to pay more for cloud storage. Unfortunately, conventional photo apps display all of your content in a grid and typically don't let you sort your library, making it hard to clear your storage efficiently. 

SnapSweeper displays your photos in a card-like fashion, swiping to the left marks a photo for deletion, and swiping a photo to the right skips to the next piece of content in your library. This allows the user to quickly act on each piece of content, while also displaying a large preview, so everything stays on one screen.

# Main Page - Design
When I started to design SnapSweeper, I knew that as a photo manager, users would also want to quickly view statistics regarding how much of what type of content are stored in their camera roll. After various redesigns, I decided to emphasize these options by creating a single main page, with buttons to select media types like 'live photos' and 'videos', displaying the current count of that media type.
