## Troubleshooting and hardships
> Storage is expensive!

One major challenge that I faced and continue to face throughout this project is finding parts. Due to the Lenovo ThinkCentre being a mini-pc, there's only one full size SATA port and one NVME slot on the motherboard. That meant almost all of my external storage would have to come from the USB ports. Luckily the ThinkCentre has six 3.0 ports, allowing me to find a second-hand SATA-RAID system.
