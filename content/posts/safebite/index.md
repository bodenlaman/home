## Find Nearby Places or Enter Your Own Preferences

SafeBite's intuitive design helps you find restaurants around you, or matching your specific craving. Using your device's MapKit framework, SafeBite uses your location to suggest the closest restaurants to you with buttons to filter by your desired category.
